Industry focus

Payments and fintech at the core. Open to any digital-first business.

Our key expertise is software for payment and financial organisations: transactional user journeys, personal accounts, onboarding and back-office tools. The same engineering approach works for other businesses with complex digital operations.

  • Payment and fintech companies
  • Processing and infrastructure providers

  • Digital-first businesses with complex operations
