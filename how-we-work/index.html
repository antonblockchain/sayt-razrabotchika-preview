How we work

A clear process with visible results at every stage

Each stage ends with a concrete result you can review. You always know what is being done, by whom and what comes next.

Stages and their results

  1. 1

    Discovery

    • Goals and constraints
    • User roles
    • Functional scope
    • Preliminary estimate
  2. 2

    UX and architecture

    • User flows and prototypes
    • System architecture
    • Integration scheme
    • Release plan
  3. 3

    Design and development

  4. 4

How you stay in control

Transparent delivery

Shared backlog, regular demos and progress reports in a format agreed at the start.

Documentation

Architecture, integrations and key decisions are documented as the project goes.

Security by design

Access control, data protection and secure development practices from the first sprint.

Quality gates

Code review, automated and manual testing before every release.

Who is on the team

The team composition depends on the task. These are the roles we usually involve.

  • Project / delivery manager
  • Business analyst
  • UX/UI designer
  • Solution architect
  • Frontend engineer
  • Backend engineer
  • Mobile engineer
  • QA engineer
  • DevOps engineer

Cooperation formats

Dedicated team

A cross-functional team that works on your product long term.

Project-based development

A defined scope delivered as a project with agreed stages.

Team extension

Our engineers strengthen your in-house team in specific areas.

Long-term support

Maintenance, monitoring and development of a product after launch.
